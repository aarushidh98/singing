Competition Journey.

Your musical journey starts with a simple online registration.

Round 1 – Online Audition

i

Register through your school.

Your name, your division, your city. One page, five minutes.

ii

Upload your singing performance.

Any genre, any language. A phone recording is enough.

iii

Expert judges will evaluate every entry.

The judges listen blind. Decisions arrive within few days.

iv

The top performers from each category will qualify for the Grand Finale.

Accepted singers are scheduled into Delhi NCR audition evening.

The shortlisted participants will perform LIVE before an esteemed panel of judges and a vibrant audience. This is where talent meets opportunity and unforgettable memories are created.
